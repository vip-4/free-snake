How to Play Snake

1. Start
Press any arrow key, Enter, or tap the board. The snake moves right by default.
2. Steer
←↑↓→ or WASD turn the snake. On touch screens, swipe in the direction you want. 180° reversals are ignored so you can't crash into yourself by accident.
3. Eat
Guide the snake to red apples — each one grows the snake by one segment and adds 10 points. Golden apples appear regularly and are worth 50.
4. Survive
The snake speeds up with every apple. Hitting a wall or your own tail ends the run — classic rules, no wrap-around. Space pauses at any time.

Snake Game FAQ

Is this snake game really free?
Yes. This online snake game is 100% free to play in any modern browser. There are no downloads, no registration, no ads, and no in-game purchases.
How do I control the snake?
On desktop use the arrow keys or WASD to steer, Space to pause, and Enter to start or restart. On a phone or tablet, swipe in the direction you want the snake to go and tap the board to pause or start.
How does scoring work in this snake game?
Each red apple is worth 10 points and makes the snake grow longer. Every fifth food spawns a golden apple worth 50 points. The snake also moves faster with each apple eaten, so risk and reward rise together.
Does the snake game work on my phone?
Yes. The game is mobile-first and responsive: swipe anywhere on the board to steer and tap to pause or start, on iOS or Android. The board scales to fit your screen.
Is my high score saved?
Yes. Your best score and sound preference are saved in your own browser via localStorage. Nothing is uploaded, no account is needed, and no personal data is collected.
What happens when the snake hits a wall or itself?
The run ends immediately — classic rules with no wrap-around. Your score and best score are shown, and you can press Enter or tap the board to start a fresh run.
